When I moved to a new city for work, the job was the easy part. Evenings and weekends were quiet in a way I had not expected.

## Saying yes to small things

I made a rule to say yes to small invitations: a lunch with colleagues, a weekend workshop, a walking group I found on a notice board. None of them were life-changing on their own.

Over a few months, familiar faces started to appear. The same people came to the workshop, and some of them knew people from the walking group. A circle slowly formed.

The city feels like home now. Looking back, it was not one big moment but many small ones, each a little easier than the last.

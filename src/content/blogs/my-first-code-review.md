The first time I opened a pull request at work, I refreshed the page every few minutes. When the comments finally arrived there were a lot of them, and my first reaction was to treat each one as a mark against me.

## Reading comments as questions

A teammate suggested I read every comment as a question rather than a verdict. Some were about naming, some about edge cases I had not thought of, and a few were simply preferences. Sorting them that way made the list feel much smaller.

I also learned to reply to each comment, even with a short "done" or "good point, changed". It kept the conversation moving and showed the reviewer what I had understood.

Now I look forward to reviews. They are the quickest way I know to learn how a team thinks about its code.

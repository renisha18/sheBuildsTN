For a long time I said yes to everything: extra tasks at work, every family function, every favour a friend asked. I thought being reliable meant being available all the time.

## A no is also an answer

The turning point was realising that each yes I gave half-heartedly was not fair to anyone. I started answering requests with a pause, and asking myself whether I had the time and energy to do it well.

Some of my answers came with an alternative: a later date, a smaller part of the task or a person better suited to help. Most people were more understanding than I expected.

My evenings now have room for rest, and the things I do agree to get my full attention. That feels far more reliable than before.

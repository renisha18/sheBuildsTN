For my first two years at work I never asked about pay. I assumed that if I did good work, someone would notice and bring it up for me.

## Writing it down

A senior colleague suggested I keep a simple list of what I had worked on and what had changed because of it. When review season came, that list became the backbone of my conversation.

I practised saying my request out loud with a friend until it stopped feeling awkward. I also prepared for a "not right now" and asked what I would need to show to get there.

The conversation was calmer than I had imagined. Whatever the outcome, I now know that asking is part of the job, not a favour.

I stepped away from work to care for my family, and when I decided to return, the gap on my CV felt bigger than the years I had worked before it.

## Telling the story plainly

I stopped trying to hide the break. In interviews I explained it in one or two sentences and moved on to what I wanted to do next. Most interviewers simply nodded.

Before applying, I spent a few months catching up: short courses, reading and small projects to remind myself how much I already knew. Talking to people who had returned before me helped most of all.

The first weeks back were tiring, and I made peace with being slower at first. Within a few months, the break felt like part of my story rather than a hole in it.

The first community meetup I attended, I stood outside the venue for a good ten minutes. I did not know anyone, and I was sure everyone else would.

## Small conversations

Inside, it turned out that many people had come alone. Someone asked what had brought me there, and that one question started a conversation that lasted the whole evening.

A few of us swapped numbers and started meeting on weekends to learn together. We were at very different stages, which made it easy to help each other.

I still feel nervous walking into a new room. But now I know that the people inside are often hoping someone will say hello first, so I try to be that person.

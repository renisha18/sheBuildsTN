When I started selling handmade jewellery, I felt I had to post every day. Within a month I was spending more time on my phone than at my workbench.

## One afternoon a week

Now I set aside one afternoon each week. I photograph the pieces I have made, write a few short captions and schedule them. The rest of the week, I only reply to messages.

I also stopped chasing every trend. Instead I share the same few things my customers enjoy: new pieces, a look at the making process and the occasional story behind a design.

Posting less often has not hurt the business. It has made the posts better, and it has given me my evenings back.

On my first data project I was eager to train something. A mentor asked me to wait and spend an hour simply reading the data, one row at a time.

## What the rows showed

That hour changed the plan. Some columns had values typed in different formats, a few dates were clearly wrong and one category had been spelled three different ways. None of this was visible from a summary table.

Cleaning those problems took longer than training the model, but the results made far more sense afterwards. It also gave me questions to ask the people who had collected the data.

Now every project starts the same way for me: open the file, scroll, take notes, and only then think about models.

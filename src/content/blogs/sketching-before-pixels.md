When I started designing, I opened a design tool the moment I got a brief. My screens looked neat, but they all looked like the first idea I had.

## Quantity first

Now I begin with paper. I fold a sheet into eight boxes and give myself a few minutes to fill each one with a different layout. Most of them are not good, and that is the point.

Paper makes it cheap to throw ideas away. Nobody worries about alignment or colour on a quick sketch, so the conversation stays on what the screen should do.

By the time I open a design tool, I already know which two or three ideas are worth building properly. The work goes faster, and the result is usually better.
